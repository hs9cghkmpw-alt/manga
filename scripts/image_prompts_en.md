# Image Generation Prompts (English)

English-language equivalents of `scripts/image_prompts.md`, for use with image models that
perform better with English prompts. Same page numbering and source material
(`scripts/00_style_guide.md`, `scripts/full_script.md`).

## Global prefix (prepend to every page prompt)

```
Full-color Japanese educational-manga style (similar to Benesse "Shinken Zemi" learning manga),
clean medium-weight digital linework, digital cel-style coloring, multi-panel page composition,
characters always placed inside a fully rendered background/scene (never a floating icon portrait),
emotion conveyed through facial expression, posture, and panel composition,
must NOT look like a PowerPoint slide, brochure, or icon+textbox infographic.
IMPORTANT: do not render any Japanese text, speech-bubble lettering, or narration text in the image.
Generate only panel layout, backgrounds, characters, and empty bubble/caption-box shapes —
all Japanese text will be typeset afterward with a proper font.
Speech bubbles = solid outline ellipse (empty). Inner-thought bubbles = cloud-shaped or dashed
outline (empty). Narration = rectangular caption box (empty). Diagram/explanation panels =
dotted-line box (empty).
```

Once `assets/reference/style_reference.jpg` is available, use it as an image/style reference
in addition to the text prompt below (currently unavailable — text-only prompting for now).

### P00 Title page
A large panel: the present-day Yuta Shiroyama (adult man, calm casual clothes) standing by a
window, thoughtful but calm expression, a faint translucent light-film "mask" effect over one
side of his face. Below, three small silhouette panels hinting at childhood / military / present
day. Warm, subdued palette.

### P01 Childhood — among brothers
Panel 1: a lively Japanese family living room, a young boy (Yuta, age ~7-9) playing energetically
with his three younger brothers. Panel 2: mother scolding Yuta (ordinary parental scolding, not
abusive). Panel 3: one younger brother clinging affectionately to Yuta. Warm indoor lighting.

### P02 Age 7 — the first separation
Panel 1: a peaceful family dinner with both parents and grandparents (flashback tone, slightly
desaturated). Panel 2: young Yuta peeking from a hallway at adults talking with serious faces.
Panel 3: Yuta's small back standing alone in front of a new elementary school gate. Panel 4: Yuta
looking down in an unfamiliar classroom. Overall cool, lonely palette.

### P03 Elementary school — what he's good at
Panel 1: classroom, young Yuta raising his hand first during a math lesson. Panel 2: Yuta at his
desk at home, working on problem sets late at night (a wall clock shows a late hour). Panel 3:
family gently telling him to go to sleep, a warm small moment. Expression: absorbed, alive with
focus.

### P04 Elementary school — struggles and workaround
Panel 1: Yuta panicking in the morning because a handout is missing. Panel 2: a teacher pointing
out a forgotten item (everyday tone, not harsh). Panel 3: Yuta stuffing all his textbooks into an
overstuffed school bag. Panel 4: Yuta walking to school with a slightly proud expression.

### P05 Junior high — lively days and reading the real rules
Panel 1: middle-school Yuta (school uniform) making classmates laugh. Panel 2: Yuta looking
relaxed in front of a stack of unsubmitted assignments. Panel 3: copying a friend's notebook at
the last minute before a deadline. Panel 4: Yuta checking a watch with a confident look (he knows
the homeroom teacher won't arrive yet). Panel 5: a friend waking him up in the morning. Bright,
energetic tone overall.

### P06 Age 12 — the second separation
Panel 1: tense atmosphere at home (avoid depicting violence directly; use suggestive imagery —
broken tableware, a flinching back). Panel 2: young Yuta (about 12) stepping protectively in
front of his mother. Panel 3: mother and son walking away from the house at night, seen from
behind. Panel 4: Yuta's complicated expression later — relief and unresolved pain together. Low
saturation, cool tones.

### P07 The broken attempt at a father-son relationship
Panel 1: young Yuta awkwardly approaching a stepfather figure. Panel 2: close-up of Yuta trying
and failing to say "dad." Panel 3: a silent, cold dinner table (visual cue of a breaking
relationship). Panel 4: Yuta's back as he moves to yet another new school, carrying his belongings.

### P08 "I have to hold it together" — the mask begins
Panel 1 (large panel): the mother saying "help me" to Yuta — a pivotal, quiet moment. Panel 2:
Yuta's face receiving those words, the childlike expression draining away for an instant. Panel 3:
Yuta's reflection in a mirror or window with a faint translucent light-film "mask" effect over his
face — this is the first explicit visual introduction of the mask motif used throughout the rest
of the story.

### P09 Struggling with self-management, and a system that fits
Panel 1: a messy desk, Yuta flustered after forgetting a plan (everyday adult/young-adult scene).
Panel 2: Yuta putting coins into a can, the lid taped shut so it can't easily be opened — visually
clear that it's deliberately hard to access. Panel 3: a time-lapse panel of the can getting
gradually heavier/fuller. Panel 4: an empty diagram/explanation panel (caption box only, text
added later).

### P10 National Defense Academy — Ground SDF Technical High School: enrollment
Panel 1: nervous new cadets lined up at the school gate, Yuta among them, in uniform. Panel 2:
early-morning roll call, a strict instructor giving orders. Panel 3: several small panels showing
tough physical training. Hard-edged khaki/gray palette.

### P11 The structure of shared responsibility
Panel 1: a classmate's mistake is discovered and the whole group springs into action. Panel 2:
Yuta's own mistake being covered by peers, depicted as a mutual, equal dynamic. Panel 3: after
solving the problem together, tired but satisfied expressions — a strong sense of camaraderie.

### P12 How he relates to rules
Panel 1: Yuta calmly but firmly following regulations. Panel 2: Yuta sternly calling out a rule
violation he witnesses. Panel 3: Yuta arguing back logically against an unreasonable order, an
older superior visibly annoyed. Panel 4: Yuta's frustrated expression after being called
"impertinent."

### P13 Communications work — a problem effort can fix
Panel 1: Yuta in SDF uniform, focused, working on communications equipment. Panel 2: trouble
occurs — warning lights, urgency. Panel 3: the team working together to restore communications.
Panel 4: after recovery, Yuta taking notes with a frustrated-but-determined expression.

### P14 Reassignment — what wasn't handed down
Panel 1: Yuta receiving a transfer order. Panel 2: Yuta alone in front of incomplete handover
documents, the previous person already gone. Panel 3: a kind first supervisor patiently teaching
him — a reassuring scene.

### P15 Caught in the middle — the gears start to break
Panel 1: a new supervisor takes over; the mood visibly shifts. Panel 2: the new supervisor
pressuring Yuta even though he isn't being allowed to make progress — the unfairness should read
clearly in the contrast of expressions. Panel 3: several stacked small panels — night shifts,
disaster response, being called in during nap/rest time, no time to recover. Panel 4: Yuta's
exhausted face, the "mask" effect visibly cracking/peeling.

### P16 The period of psychological breakdown
Panel 1: a quiet, desaturated panel — Yuta sitting alone, curled inward. Panel 2: a
semi-transparent flashback panel of his earlier effort (echoing P13). Panel 3: an empty
diagram/explanation panel. Overall the most desaturated, heavy tone in the book — but do NOT draw
him as someone requiring constant physical care.

### P17 What is "brain fatigue"? (explanation page)
Panel 1: a diagram panel (empty caption box) showing icons for "conversation," "schedules,"
"relationships," "unanswerable questions" stacking up inside a head silhouette. Panel 2: Yuta
gradually wearing down while giving or receiving counsel in a conversation with no clear answer.
Panel 3: close-up of his face nearing his limit. Panel 4: a large panel with generous empty space
reserved for emphasis lettering.

### P18 Career after the SDF — the strength that keeps reappearing
Panels 1-5: a chronological sequence of small panels — SDF (comms/maintenance) → consulting
(hearing out a client) → web design/IT (teaching beginners) → telecom/network (base-station,
network equipment work) → employment-transition support (a counseling meeting). Each panel
repeats the same core gestures: listening, organizing, explaining.

### P19 From supporting others to being supported
Panel 1: Yuta, in the past, as an employment-support staff member counseling a user (the
supporting role). Panel 2: a scene transition — now Yuta is the one being interviewed/counseled
(a deliberate visual contrast/mirroring layout). Panel 3: Yuta's expression as he registers this
reversal.

### P20 Outlets — looking for a replacement
Panel 1: a flashback bar scene (not moralized, just past reality). Panel 2: overlapping with a
scene from his current life. Panel 3: an empty diagram panel outlining the qualities he's looking
for in a new outlet. Panel 4: a scene left deliberately open-ended, as an unanswered question.

### P21 Family and hobbies — more than a support recipient
Panel 1: a warm family dinner/gathering with his wife and child. Panel 2: night fishing in
Otaru, happily reeling in mackerel or other blue-back fish. Panel 3: Yuta at a shogi board (a
recently started hobby). Panel 4: Yuta at a PC, taking notes on an app idea or a personal
information-organizing system. Panel 5: a small panel of Yuta's complicated expression while
thinking about his mother. Overall warm palette returning.

### P22 Understanding over caregiving (key late-story theme)
Panel 1: Yuta deliberately declining help (asserting he wants to do it himself). Panel 2: a
moment where he's truly out of capacity and can't even ask for help — falls silent (a deliberate
contrast with earlier scenes). Panel 3: a supportive figure (representing a visiting nurse or
support worker) keeping an appropriate distance while watching over him. Panel 4: an empty
diagram panel listing five response options — "speak up / wait / organize information / adjust
the schedule / do nothing." The "mask" effect should be visibly softened in this page.

### P23 Things to keep thinking about together
Panel 1: a symbolic gathering — support staff, medical staff, family, and Yuta around the same
table. Panel 2: an empty diagram panel for an 11-item list (text typeset later).

### P24 The future — living a life with understanding around him
Panel 1: Yuta engaged positively at his current workplace/support program. Panel 2: an imagined
future scene of him passing on his own coping strategies to someone else (clearly a lighter,
slightly softer "not yet, but hoped-for" tone). Panel 3 (final large panel): Yuta surrounded by
family, work, hobbies, and supporters — the "mask" effect fully removed, natural unguarded
expression. The warmest palette in the book, as the closing image.
